made boring · brand mark → wordmark · 4 ideas

The mark should become the domain.

Jira, Atlassian and Slack all build their icon from a few primitives that can come apart and reassemble — which is exactly what makes them animate well. Same principle here, with one extra move: the mark is not next to the wordmark, it is a letter inside it. Press replay on any of these.

01 · The O — recommended

madebring.com

The bored ring lands as the “o”

The icon is a character from the domain, enlarged — the same trick that lets a single shape stand in for the whole name. It arrives as a big bored ring, then shrinks into its slot and the rest of the letters fill in around it. The favicon is just the ring.

02 · The Fold

madeboring.com

The chevron flattens into the rule

The mark you liked, doing the thing the brand promises: it folds itself flat and becomes a plain horizontal line under the name. Complexity collapsing into a straight edge, literally.

03 · The Collapse

madeboring.com

Four bars stack down into one line of type

Steps being removed, one per bar, until what is left is the name. The most literal statement of the service, and the only one of the four that would also work as a loading state inside the product.

04 · The Assembly

madeboring.com

Three segments fly in and nest

Straight out of the Jira and Atlassian playbook — one shape repeated at three tints, arriving from three directions and locking together, then the wordmark slides out from behind it. The most conventional of the four, and the most obviously “a tech company”.

All four are pure CSS on real geometry — no video, a few KB, sharp at any size, and they hold still for anyone browsing with reduced motion switched on.
