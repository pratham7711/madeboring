made boring · brandround six · MB · 8 monograms

Two letters that unfold
into the whole name.

MB buys you the one thing a mark living inside the word never could: it detaches. It is an app icon, an avatar, a favicon, a stamp on an invoice, and a symbol that sits beside the wordmark rather than trapped in the middle of it. Every option below is shown in exactly that lockup. And because m and b really are the first letters of made and boring, the monogram can expand into the full name without cheating — which is what the first one does.

The honest risk, stated once: MB is contested territory. Every second consultancy is an MB, and the shortform carries no meaning of its own — with a single exception, which is that MB is already a unit of data. Option 07 is built on exactly that, and it is the only one of the eight that arrives with a meaning already attached rather than one you have to spend money teaching people. Options 02 and 04 are the ones that solve the sameness problem by construction instead: a merged stem and a coloured slash are both things a generic MB does not have.
